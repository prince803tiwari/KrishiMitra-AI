# 🌾 KrishiMitra AI - Demo Video Script (Easy Version)

This script is written in **simple, easy-to-speak English** and is designed to help you record a smooth, under-4-minute demo video of your website.

## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording:
1. **Speak Slowly**: Take a deep breath between sections. Don't rush!
2. **Use a Pointer**: Move your mouse cursor to highlight the buttons and text boxes as you talk about them.
3. **Pre-load Files**: Have a sample tomato or crop leaf image ready on your desktop so you can drag-and-drop it quickly.
4. **Volume Check**: Ensure your microphone is clear and there is no background noise before recording.
